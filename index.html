About This Event

Hosted in partnership with the University of the Incarnate Word

This piano event offers instruments similar to those loaned to the University of the Incarnate Word Department of Music. Alamo Music Center, UIW's authorized retail partner, is presenting these instruments to the public with reduced pricing available by appointment.

Event Hosted By
Alamo Music Center
University of the Incarnate Word's Authorized Retail Partner

UIW's School of Music has a special arrangement with Kawai America Corporation that directly benefits the university's music students and faculty. This program provides UIW with quality new pianos each year. Models similar to those used by the university are now being offered to the public at greatly reduced prices.

About this event: Alamo Music Center is the seller of these instruments. The University of the Incarnate Word has an institutional piano program in partnership with Kawai and Alamo Music Center. UIW does not directly sell, endorse, or sponsor this advertisement.

✦ The Official Letter ✦

From the University of the Incarnate Word Department of Music

Official UIW Department of Music Letter

Dear Friends,

I am pleased to notify you that many fine pianos similar to those loaned to the University of the Incarnate Word are now available for purchase. The selection includes Kawai baby grands, concert grands, uprights, studios, console and performance-quality digital pianos, as well as a large selection of pre-owned pianos including Yamaha, Kawai, and Steinway uprights and grands.

Our School of Music has a special arrangement that directly benefits our talented music students and faculty. This agreement, made with Kawai America Corporation, provides UIW with quality new pianos each year. These models are now being offered to the public at greatly reduced prices.

All pianos will first be offered to recipients of this letter, alumni, and friends, at reduced prices by appointment before being sold to the general public.

These pianos have been professionally maintained and most include the original ten-year manufacturer's warranty.

By Appointment Only: Thursday through Saturday, September 24-26. Arrange an appointment by calling (210) 888-0181 or booking online below.

General Public Day: Sunday, September 27 from noon to 5pm at Alamo Music Center in San Antonio.

— Dr. Orit Amy Eylon, Chair
Department of Music, University of the Incarnate Word
